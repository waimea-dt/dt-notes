# Week 3: Data, UI & Scenes

This week you'll learn how to store lots of information as **data**, how to build a **UI** that can show any of it, and how to split a game into separate **scenes**. Then you'll use all of it to build a card battle game where every card is just a bundle of numbers.


| Lesson | Title                                                              | You will learn                                                                           |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | [Lists & Objects](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson1.md)         | Arrays, objects, indexes, looking up properties, `for...of`                              |
| 2      | [Displaying Data](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson2.md)         | Building a UI from objects, parents and children, a view you create once and update      |
| 3      | [Dealing Cards](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson3.md)           | Showing items from an array, random picks, building your own deck                        |
| 4      | [Creature Clash](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson4.md)          | Scenes, passing data, and building your own card battle game                             |


By the end of the week you will have designed and built your own card battle game. Here's one made with the creatures in the starter project. Pick a stat, and the biggest number wins!
