# Week 3: Dialogue & Storylines

This week you'll learn how to store a whole story as **data**, how to split a game into separate **scenes**, and how to let the player make **choices** that change what happens next.


| Lesson | Title                                                              | You will learn                                                                              |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | [Lists & Objects](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson1.md)         | Arrays, objects, indexes, showing dialogue on screen                                        |
| 2      | [Scenes](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson2.md)                  | `scene`, `go`, passing data between scenes, where variables live                            |
| 3      | [Branching Stories](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson3.md)       | Story nodes, choice buttons built from data, flags, different endings                       |
| 4      | [The Wizard's Help Desk](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson4.md)  | `import` / `export`, the typewriter effect, portraits, building a full game                 |


By the end of the week you will have a working game: The Wizard's Help Desk. You'll visit a wizard who runs a very unusual IT support desk, and your choices will decide how your computer's ghostly problem ends!
