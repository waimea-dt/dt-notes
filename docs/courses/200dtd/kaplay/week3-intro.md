# Week 3: Data & Displaying Data

This week you'll learn how to store lots of information as **data**, using arrays and objects, and how to build a **UI** that can show any of it. You'll finish by building a viewer for a deck of cards of your own design.


| Lesson | Title                                                              | You will learn                                                                           |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | [Arrays](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson1.md)                  | Lists of values, indexes, `length`, `push`, `for...of`                                   |
| 2      | [Objects](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson2.md)                 | Bundles of named values, arrays of objects, looking up properties with a variable        |
| 3      | [Data in the UI](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson3.md)     | Building a UI from objects, parents and children, a view you create once and update      |
| 4      | [Deck Viewer](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson4.md)     | Showing items from an array, an index, buttons, random picks, your own deck              |


By the end of the week you will have built a viewer for a deck of cards of your own. Here's one made with the creatures in the starter project. Click the buttons to flick through the deck!
