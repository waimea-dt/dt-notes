# Week 3: Data, Scenes & Cards

This week you'll learn how to store lots of information as **data**, how to split a game into separate **scenes**, and how to use both to build a card battle game where every card is just a bundle of numbers.


| Lesson | Title                                                              | You will learn                                                                           |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | [Lists & Objects](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson1.md)         | Arrays, objects, indexes, looking up properties, showing cards on screen                 |
| 2      | [Scenes](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson2.md)                  | `scene`, `go`, passing data between scenes, where variables live                         |
| 3      | [Card Battle](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson3.md)             | Comparing data, picking random cards, rounds, scoring across scenes                      |
| 4      | [Creature Clash](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week3-lesson4.md)          | Designing your own deck, stats and rules (your game, your data!)                         |


By the end of the week you will have designed and built your own card battle game. Here's one made with the creatures in the starter project. Pick a stat, and the biggest number wins!
