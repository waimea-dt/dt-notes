# Week 6: Gravity, Physics & Platforms

This week you'll build a **platformer**: a player who runs, jumps and falls, through levels full of coins and spikes. You'll switch on **gravity**, **draw** your levels as maps made of text, and link them together into a game. Then you'll design and build a platformer of your own.


| Lesson | Title                                                              | You will learn                                                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | [Gravity & Jumping](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week6-lesson1.md)       | `setGravity`, physics bodies, static floors, `jump()`, `isGrounded()`, game feel      |
| 2      | [Level Maps](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week6-lesson2.md)              | Levels as arrays of strings, `addLevel`, tiles, designing a level on paper            |
| 3      | [Pickups & Levels](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week6-lesson3.md)        | Coins, spikes and exits, an array of levels, `currentLevel`, winning                  |
| 4      | [Build a Platformer](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week6-lesson4.md)      | Designing and building your own platformer                                            |


By the end of the week you will have built a platformer of your own. Here's **Coin Dash**, made with the pictures in the starter project. Move with the arrow keys, jump with SPACE... and mind the gaps!
