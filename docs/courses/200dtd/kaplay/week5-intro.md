# Week 5: Fly, Shoot, Explode

This week you'll build a classic **shooter**: a ship at the bottom of the screen, bugs falling from the top, and lots of explosions! You'll learn how to make bullets, how to detect when things **collide**, and how to make a game that gets **harder** as you play. Then you'll design and build a shooter of your own.


| Lesson | Title                                                              | You will learn                                                                        |
| ------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1      | [Shooting](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week5-lesson1.md)                | Moving a ship, firing bullets, `onUpdate("tag", ...)`, `clamp`, `offscreen`           |
| 2      | [Enemies & Collisions](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week5-lesson2.md)    | Spawning enemies, custom properties, `onCollide`, `isSensor`, explosions, score       |
| 3      | [Lives & Difficulty](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week5-lesson3.md)      | Lives, a `loseLife()` function, a fresh start each game, a game that gets harder      |
| 4      | [Build a Shooter](/courses/200dtd/kaplay/week5-lesson4.md)         | Designing and building your own shooter                                               |


By the end of the week you will have built a shooter of your own. Here's **Bug Blaster**, made with the pictures in the starter project. Move with the arrow keys, shoot with SPACE... and don't let the bugs get past!
