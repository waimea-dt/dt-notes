# Week 2: Building Tables

This week you'll stop asking questions and start building. You'll **create** your own tables, choose the right **data types**, and **add**, **change** and **remove** the data inside them. You'll finish by designing and building a table of your own.


| Lesson | Title | You will learn |
| ------ | ----- | -------------- |
| 1 | [CREATE TABLE](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week2-lesson1.md) | Designing a table, data types, primary keys, `NOT NULL`, `DEFAULT` |
| 2 | [INSERT](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week2-lesson2.md) | Adding rows, letting the id fill itself in, constraint errors |
| 3 | [UPDATE & DELETE](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week2-lesson3.md) | Changing and removing rows, and why `WHERE` really matters |
| 4 | [Build a Table](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week2-lesson4.md) | Designing, building and querying a table of your own |
