# Week 3: Linking Tables

One table can only take you so far. This week you'll split data into **linked tables**, connect them with **foreign keys**, and pull them back together with **JOINs**. You'll finish by designing a pair of linked tables of your own.


| Lesson | Title | You will learn |
| ------ | ----- | -------------- |
| 1 | [Foreign Keys](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week3-lesson1.md) | Repeated data, splitting tables, `REFERENCES`, one-to-many, ERDs |
| 2 | [JOIN](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week3-lesson2.md) | `JOIN ... ON`, `table.column`, aliases, joins with `WHERE` and `ORDER BY` |
| 3 | [More JOINs](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week3-lesson3.md) | Joins with `GROUP BY`, `LEFT JOIN`, a peek at many-to-many |
| 4 | [Build Linked Tables](/courses/200dtd/sqlite/week3-lesson4.md) | Designing, building and querying linked tables of your own |
