## Adventure Books

- ![](_assets/books/cryptonomicon.jpg)
    [Cryptonomicon](hhttps://www.goodreads.com/book/show/816.Cryptonomicon)

    - **Adventure**
    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **History**
    - Neal Stephenson

    Two interconnected storylines: One in WWII and one in the 1990s; linked by code breaking, conspiracies, computers, mysterious organisations, and gold... Lots of gold.

- ![](_assets/books/baroque-cycle.jpg)
    The Baroque Cycle

    - **Adventure**
    - **History**
    - **Sci-Fi**
    - Neal Stephenson

    A tale that is ambitious and epically vast in scope, crossing oceans, centuries and generations; a tale of conspiracies, royalty, science, pirates, gold, battles and maths.

    1. [Quicksilver](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/823.Quicksilver)
    1. [The Confusion](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/822.The_Confusion)
    1. [The System of the World](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/116257.The_System_of_the_World)


## Sci-Fi Books

- ![](_assets/books/diamond-age.jpg)
    [The Diamond Age](https://www.goodreads.com/en/book/show/827.The_Diamond_Age)

    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **Adventure**
    - Neal Stephenson

    A near-future novel in a world of social enclaves and nano-technology; a young girl comes upon an artificially intelligent book that will change her life and those around her.

- ![](_assets/books/hhgttg.avif)
    The Hitchhikers Guide to the Galaxy

    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **Comedy**
    - **Adventure**
    - Douglas Adams

    An amusing romp around the galaxy with robots, aliens, whales, planetary destruction, petunias and towels. Uniquely silly humour from Douglas Adams.

    1. [The Hitchhikers Guide to the Galaxy](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/11.The_Hitchhiker_s_Guide_to_the_Galaxy)
    1. [The Restaurant at the End of the Universe](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/8695.The_Restaurant_at_the_End_of_the_Universe)
    1. [Life, the Universe and Everything](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/8694.Life_the_Universe_and_Everything)
    1. [So Long, and Thanks for All the Fish](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/6091075-so-long-and-thanks-for-all-the-fish)
    1. [Mostly Harmless](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/569429.Mostly_Harmless)

- ![](_assets/books/seveneves.jpg)
    [Seveneves](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/22816087-seveneves)

    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **Thriller**
    - **Adventure**
    - Neal Stephenson

    The world faces a catastrophic, unavoidable end, and races against time to save some of humanity. It all goes well... until it doesn't.

- ![](_assets/books/dune.jpg)
    Dune

    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **Adventure**
    - **War**
    - **Drama**
    - Frank Herbert

    A planet of sand, an exotic spice, warring families and giant worms; the first volume in a series that spans millennia is gripping. Some of the later books get to be hard work, but the first few are amazing.

    1. [Dune](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/44767458-dune)
    1. [Dune Messiah](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/44492285-dune-messiah)
    1. [Children of Dune](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/44492286-children-of-dune)
    1. ... and more

- ![](_assets/books/enders-game.jpg)
    Ender's Saga

    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **Adventure**
    - Orson Scott Card

    Children trained to fight a world-threatening alien fleet, pushed beyond their limits; extinction of worlds and species; political machinations; redemption arcs... These books forse us to confront difficult questions about who we are as humans.

    1. [Ender's Game](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/375802.Ender_s_Game)
    1. [Speaker for the Dead](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7967.Speaker_for_the_Dead)
    1. [Xenocide](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/8648.Xenocide)
    1. [Children of the Mind](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/31360.Children_of_the_Mind)
    1. [Ender in Exile](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/3220405-ender-in-exile)
    1. [The Last Shadow](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7108926-the-last-shadow)

- ![](_assets/books/anathem.jpg)
    [Anathem](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/2845024-anathem)

    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **Adventure**
    - **Mystery**
    - Neal Stephenson

    Life for the fraas and suurs of the Concent of Saunt Edhar, a sanctuary for science and maths, moves to a slow and steady beat, but a threat to the world triggers an exodus across, and then off the planet.

- ![](_assets/books/culture.jpg)
    The Culture

    - **Sci-Fi**
    - **Adventure**
    - **Philosophy**

    1. [Excession](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12013.Excession)
    1. [Inversions](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12017.Inversions)
    1. [Look to Windward](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12016.Look_to_Windward)
    1. [Matter](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/886066.Matter)
    1. [Surface Detail](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/7937744-surface-detail)
    1. [The Hydrogen Sonata](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/13497991-the-hydrogen-sonata)

## Fantasy Books


- ![](_assets/books/dark-materials.jpg)
    His Dark Materials

    - **Fantasy**
    - **Adventure**
    - **Drama**
    - Philip Pullman

    Parallel worlds touch and vast powers battle for control; armoured bears, witches, daemons, innocence, betrayal, love, sacrifice... These books have it all.

    1. [Northern Lights](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/70947.Northern_Lights)
    2. [The Subtle Knife](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/41637836-the-subtle-knife)
    3. [The Amber Spyglass](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/18122.The_Amber_Spyglass)


- ![](_assets/books/lotr.jpg)
    Middle Earth

    - **Fantasy**
    - **Adventure**
    - J.R.R. Tolkien

    A ring, some hobbits, a wizard, a dragons, many battles, many tragedies, true friendship... These books and the world they create are unforgettable.

    1. [The Hobbit](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/5907.The_Hobbit_or_There_and_Back_Again)
    2. [The Fellowship of the Ring](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/61215351-the-fellowship-of-the-ring)
    3. [The Two Towers](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/61215372-the-two-towers)
    4. [The Return of the King](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/61215384-the-return-of-the-king)


- ![](_assets/books/good-omens.jpg)
    [Good Omens](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12067.Good_Omens)

    - **Fantasy**
    - **Comedy**
    - Terry Pratchett, Neil Gaiman

    'The Nice and Accurate Prophecies of Agnes Nutter, Witch' - But how accurate? The apocalypse is next Saturday, armies of Good and Evil are massing, and the Antichrist is missing...


- ![](_assets/books/graveyard.jpg)
    [The Graveyard Book](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/2213661.The_Graveyard_Book)

    - **Fantasy**
    - **Adventure**
    - Neil Gaiman

    Nobody Owens lives in a graveyard, raised by the dead. But now he's a little older, the world of the living is calling him... But is it safe?


- ![](_assets/books/strange-norrell.jpg)
    [Jonathan Strange & Mr. Norrell](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/14201.Jonathan_Strange_Mr_Norrell)

    - **Fantasy**
    - **Adventure**
    - Susanna Clarke

    An alternative 19th Century world where magic still exists, but long forgotten. One magician could win the war...


- ![](_assets/books/dark-tower.jpg)
    The Dark Tower

    - **Fantasy**
    - **Adventure**
    - Stephen King

    An epic and gripping tale of many worlds, linked an dying as the power of the Dark Tower wains. A gunslinger and a boy journey to save it.

    2. [The Drawing of the Three](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/5094.The_Drawing_of_the_Three)
    3. [The Waste Lands](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/34084.The_Waste_Lands)
    4. [Wizard and Glass](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/5096.Wizard_and_Glass)
    5. [The Wind Through the Keyhole](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/12341557-the-wind-through-the-keyhole)
    6. [Wolves of the Calla](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/4978.Wolves_of_the_Calla)
    7. [Song of Sussanah](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/5093.Song_of_Susannah)
    8. [The Dark Tower](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/5091.The_Dark_Tower)
